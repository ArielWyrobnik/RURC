01 — About

A home for everyone who wants to build.

We started RURC with a simple belief: the best way to learn robotics is to build a robot. Not to read about one — to wire it, break it, debug it at 2 a.m., and watch it finally move.

We're an open, cross-disciplinary community — computer science, business, law, design and beyond. No prior experience required, only curiosity and the willingness to get your hands dirty. Members learn by doing, ship real projects, compete, and grow into the engineers, founders and leaders robotics needs next.

  • ↳ Hands-on, project-first learning
  • ↳ Open to every faculty & skill level
  • ↳ Workshops, build nights & competitions
  • ↳ A bridge to industry and research

02 — What we do

Four ways to get your hands on a robot.

Every member joins a team. Teams own a real goal for the year — and the freedom to reach it however they want.

01

Autonomous Ground Robots

Mobile platforms that sense, map and navigate on their own — SLAM, path planning and real-time control on hardware we build and tune ourselves.

ROS 2SLAMControl
02

Aerial Robotics & Drones

From first hover to autonomous flight. We design airframes, tune flight controllers and add perception so our drones can think for themselves.

Flight CtrlPerceptionPX4
03

AI & Perception

Teaching machines to see and decide. Computer vision, sensor fusion and learned policies that turn raw data into smart, real-world action.

VisionMLSensor Fusion
04

Mechatronics & Makers

Where ideas become hardware. CAD, 3D printing, PCB design and embedded firmware — the craft of making mechanical, electrical and software parts move as one.

CADPCBEmbedded
